## What’s New?

### Research Section  
Explore our updated <a href="https://kamalasaurus.github.io/bergelsonlab.org/research/">Research</a> section featuring new project highlights that reflect the key scientific questions we pursue:

- **What are the factors shaping the composition and function of microbiomes?**  
- **What drives the evolution and diversification of NLR genes in *Arabidopsis* species?**  
- **How is extensive strain diversity maintained in *Pseudomonas syringae*?**

These questions guide our work across diverse systems like *Arabidopsis thaliana* and rice, where we:

- Uncover the ecological and genetic drivers of host-associated microbial communities  
- Dissect the evolutionary dynamics of plant immune receptors (NLRs) using genomic and population-scale approaches  
- Investigate how co-infection, immune responses, and environmental structure shape pathogen diversity

Our research integrates controlled experiments, synthetic communities, multi-omics, and theory to understand host-microbe interactions from genotype to ecosystem.
